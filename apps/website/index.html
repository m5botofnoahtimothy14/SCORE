How Mentor Desk works

From doubt submission to verified session closure.

1. Submit a doubt

Pick your subject, ask your question, and get matched with an assigned mentor.

2. Book a session

Choose 15 or 30 minutes, pick a time, and get a Google Meet link instantly.

3. Verify & close

Share the OTP with your mentor at session end — they verify it to close the session.

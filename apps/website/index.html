Built for how schools actually work

Everything a school needs to communicate safely and effectively.

Mentor Desk

Students submit doubts, teachers claim and resolve them, with weekly quotas and urgent passes.

Announcements

School-wide or class-targeted notices with attachments and read tracking.

Live Sessions

Book 15/30-minute sessions with Google Meet links and OTP-verified closure.

Chat History

Every student-teacher conversation is stored, tracked, and auditable.

Student-Teacher Chat

Each doubt becomes a conversation thread. Students and teachers exchange messages, attachments, and resolutions in real time.

  • ✓ Full message history with timestamps
  • ✓ Read/unread tracking per user
  • ✓ Attachment support (images, documents)
  • ✓ Moderated and safeguarded

Doubt Lifecycle

Each doubt is a tracked token — submitted, assigned, in-progress, answered, closed. Teachers resolve; students rate.

  • ✓ Status tracking (pending → answered → closed)
  • ✓ Reopen and follow-up support
  • ✓ Teacher assessments and student ratings
  • ✓ Full audit trail in database

Meeting History

Every session is tracked — from booking to OTP-verified closure.

Session Booking

Students book 15 or 30-minute sessions. Teachers confirm with scheduling. Google Meet links auto-generated.

  • ✓ 15 & 30-minute durations
  • ✓ Weekly allowance tracking
  • ✓ Google Meet calendar events

OTP Closure

At session end, student gets an OTP. Teacher enters it to verify and close the session. 30-min expiry, 5-attempt lock.

  • ✓ Student receives 6-digit OTP
  • ✓ Teacher verifies to close session
  • ✓ No-show reporting & refunds

Meeting History

All sessions logged with status, duration, actual minutes used, and verification timestamps.

  • ✓ Status tracking (booked → completed)
  • ✓ Actual vs reserved minutes
  • ✓ Allowance refund on cancellation

AI Manager

Control app UI, styling, text visibility, database, and app management — all from the web.

UI & Styling

Adjust colors, text sizes, card radius, dark/light mode, and visual themes across the app.

Database Management

Manage subjects, students, teachers, sessions, and curriculum data from the web dashboard.

App Management

Configure school settings, mentor desk policies, session allowances, and notification rules.

Security first, by design

  • ✓ Fail-closed content moderation
  • ✓ School-level data isolation
